# FuturePath O'Level curriculum alignment plan

Last reviewed: 15 November 2026

## Important distinction

FuturePath currently has the 25-subject UTME catalogue and 288 topic records. That is a useful UTME study map, but it is **not yet a verified, complete WAEC/NECO O'Level syllabus**. A JAMB subject catalogue and a WAEC/NECO subject catalogue are related but not identical.

For the 2026–2027 WAEC Nigeria registration information, WAEC lists subjects including English Language, General Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Agriculture, Geography, Government, CRS, Islamic Studies, Nigerian History, languages, French, Arabic, Visual Art, Music, Literature-in-English, Home Management, Accounting, Commerce and Economics. WAEC also notes that Citizenship and Heritage Studies and Digital Technologies are not examined until 2028. The official WAEC subject list and current exam-board syllabus must be checked before labelling any topic as examinable.

## Required audit before claiming full O'Level coverage

1. Import the current official WAEC syllabus for every applicable subject and the current NECO syllabus where available.
2. Compare each syllabus topic and objective against FuturePath's topic catalogue.
3. Add missing topics, practicals, experiments, map work, prescribed texts and assessment objectives.
4. Mark each topic with exam-board tags: JAMB UTME, WAEC, NECO, or more than one.
5. Keep syllabus year/version and source reference on each subject.
6. Review the crosswalk whenever an examination body changes its syllabus.
