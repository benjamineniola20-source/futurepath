Question: Which part of a cell contains most of its genetic material?
A: Nucleus
B: Ribosome
C: Cell membrane
D: Cytoplasm
Answer: A
Year: 2023
Explanation: The nucleus contains most of the cell's DNA and helps control cell activities.

Question: Which process do green plants use to manufacture food?
A: Respiration
B: Photosynthesis
C: Transpiration
D: Germination
Answer: B
Year: 2023
Explanation: Photosynthesis uses light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose.
